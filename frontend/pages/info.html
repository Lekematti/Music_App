About the Music App

Welcome to the Music App! Here you can upload, manage, and listen to your favorite songs.

Features

  • Upload your own songs with cover images.
  • Browse top tracks and new releases.
  • Listen and Rate songs.
  • Create and manage your profile.

Good to Know

  • Make sure to keep your audio files in MP3 format for the best compatibility.
  • The Covers for your song will be fitted to a 200x200 pixel size. But using images up to 4k should work fine.
  • If the site is inactive for an extended period, the server might take up to 50s to boot up.
  • The app uses supabase buckets for storage and there is a 50mb limit. So for now only a limited number of songs can be uploaded.
